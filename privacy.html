Rack Coach privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Short version: the app doesn't collect anything that identifies you. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

On your phone

The tiles you enter, your drill score and how many free drills you've used today are saved on your phone. Nothing else sees them. Delete the app and they're gone.

Purchases

Apple handles the payment, so I never see your card or your Apple ID. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. It gets a random ID the app makes up, your purchase history for Rack Coach, and basic details like the app version and which country's App Store you use. None of that is tied to your name or email. Their policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Kids

Rack Coach isn't made for children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

I'll update this page and the date at the top.
